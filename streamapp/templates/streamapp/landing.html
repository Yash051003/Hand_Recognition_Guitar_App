What We Built

Music through the movement of hands

Acoustic Hands is a real-time hand gesture recognition system that translates finger positions into guitar chords — no physical instrument needed. Using computer vision and deep learning, we track 21 hand landmarks per hand and classify them into musical gestures.

The system processes live webcam footage through a MediaPipe hand tracking pipeline, feeds the landmark data into a trained TensorFlow Lite classifier, and maps two-hand gesture combinations to a library of 24 chords, played instantly via preloaded audio.

How It Works

Three steps to sound

01
Capture

Your webcam feeds a live video stream to the browser. Frames are captured every 300ms and sent over a WebSocket connection to the Django backend.

02
Classify

MediaPipe extracts 21 3D landmarks per hand. Our TFLite model classifies each hand's gesture — fist, thumb, index, pinky, or combinations — in milliseconds.

03
Play

The two-hand gesture pair is matched against 24 chord mappings. A match triggers the corresponding audio sample — major, minor, flat, or sharp — instantly.
